# Welcome to DMotionX Organization 👋

**DMotionX** is the official open-source organization behind **DrawMotion** — a modern, free, and local-first 2D vector animation & whiteboard suite built with Python and Qt (PySide6).

---

### 🎨 Our Flagship Project
- 🖌️ **[DrawMotion](https://github.com/DMotionX/DrawMotion):** Free & open-source whiteboard animation engine (GNU GPLv3).
- 🌐 **Official Website:** [https://drawmotion-web.vercel.app](https://drawmotion-web.vercel.app)
- 💻 **Cross-Platform:** Available for Windows and Linux.

---

### 🤝 How to Get Involved
We welcome contributors, designers, translators, and creators from around the globe!
- 🐞 **Report Issues:** Found a bug? Open an issue in our repository.
- 💡 **Join Discussions:** Share your thoughts, artboards, and feature requests.
